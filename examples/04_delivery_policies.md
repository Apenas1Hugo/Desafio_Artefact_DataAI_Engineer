# Example 04 — Delivery Policies

**Feature:** Store policy retrieval using PDF-based information retrieval

**User:** How does delivery work for customers in the Campo Grande metropolitan area? Is there free shipping?

**Assistant:**

### Delivery in the Campo Grande Metropolitan Area

- **Free shipping:** Available for orders over R$500.00.
- **Orders below R$500.00:** A fixed delivery fee of R$35.00 applies.
- **Delivery time:** 1 to 3 business days.
- **Delivery method:** The store uses its own delivery service (motoboy).

Before delivery, our team contacts the customer by phone to arrange a convenient delivery time.

If you have any other questions about our delivery policies, feel free to ask!
