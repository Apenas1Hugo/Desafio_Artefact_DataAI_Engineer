# Example 05 — Payment Policies

**Feature:** Payment methods and installment conditions

**User:** What payment methods does Empório da Música accept, and what are the installment conditions?

**Assistant:**

### Accepted Payment Methods

| Payment Method | Condition | Details |
|---|---|---|
| PIX | Full payment | 5% discount on the regular price |
| Debit Card | Full payment | All major card brands accepted |
| Credit Card | Installments | Up to 12 interest-free installments |
| Bank Slip (Boleto) | Full payment | Processing within 3 business days |

### Credit Card Installment Conditions

- **Up to 3 installments:** Interest-free, with a minimum installment amount of R$50.00.
- **4 to 6 installments:** Interest-free, with a minimum installment amount of R$80.00.
- **7 to 12 installments:** Interest-free, with a minimum installment amount of R$100.00.

### Combined Payment Methods

Customers can combine payment methods (for example, PIX and credit card) for purchases over R$2,000.00.

The assistant can also help customers simulate installment payments for a specific product.
